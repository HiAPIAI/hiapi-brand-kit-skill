# Drawing the logo in SVG

- **One idea.** The mark should say one thing about the business (Ember & Oat: an oat grain whose split is a flame,
  inside the round mouth of a wood-fired oven). If you need a sentence to explain it, simplify.
- **Simple geometry.** Circles, arcs, a few cubic curves. 3–6 shapes. No gradients, no shadows, no thin hairlines.
- **Small-size test.** Render at 32 px and 1200 px. At 32 px it must still read as one clear shape.
- **Two or three colours**, all from the palette; one-colour versions must still work (white on the primary).
- **Wordmark**: a Google Font loaded inside the SVG with `@import`; tighten letter-spacing on large display type,
  open it up (0.2–0.3em) on small capitals. Check the text never runs past the viewBox (render and look).
- **Lockups**: primary (mark left, name right), stacked (mark above name), mark only. Keep the mark-to-text gap about
  half the mark's width.
- **Clear space** around every lockup ≥ a feature of the mark (e.g. the stem height).
- Don't imitate existing logos; avoid the obvious cliché of the category unless you twist it.
